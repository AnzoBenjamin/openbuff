## [MEDIUM] api-contract — agents/base2/base2.ts:2927 — CONFIRMED Q1: finding ids are reviewer-supplied free-form strings with an RF-<n>-<fnv8> fallback; the id itself encodes NO reviewer kind
- **Risk:** Id patterns like code-reviewer:<file>:<slug>, sec-tokencounter:..., compatibility-reviewer:compatibility:stale-snapshot are conventions chosen by the spawned reviewer, not a gate-structured namespace: the gate adopts the reviewer's id verbatim (`id: record?.id ?? buildReviewerFindingId(text, index)` at base2.ts:2927 (security), 3762 (specialist), 5677 and 5787 (code-reviewer)) and gate-reviewer.ts:942 normalizes it as free text (`const id = typeof item.id === 'string' ? item.id.trim() : ''`).
