## [MEDIUM] state-mutation — docs/request-flow.md:1 — Q-split: the documented contract is the RUNTIME-owned gate; base2.ts carries a SECOND inline copy of gate-reviewer/fingerprint/paths helpers inside handleSteps' <gate-helpers-generated> region (must be kept in sync by a generator, not hand-edited)
